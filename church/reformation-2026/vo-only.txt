A storm. A dirt road. A young law student, alone.

Lightning strikes so close it throws him to the ground. Martin Luther cries out: "Saint Anne, help me! I will become a monk!"

He kept that vow. Prayed, fasted, confessed for hours — and was still terrified. How could a sinner stand before a holy God?

Then he found it in Romans: "The righteous shall live by faith." He'd been climbing toward God. God had already come down — in Jesus. Not earned. Not bought. Given.

October 31, 1517. He nails ninety-five theses to a church door in Wittenberg. The printing press does the rest.

Soon ordinary people had a Bible in their own language — and met Jesus in it for themselves.
